 Final Business Insights

Based on the exploratory analysis and demand forecasting results:

1. Historical sales data shows clear demand patterns across different stores and products.

2. Sales vary significantly by day of the week, with higher average demand observed toward the weekend.

3. Monthly sales analysis indicates the presence of seasonal demand patterns.

4. Store-level analysis shows that demand is not evenly distributed across all stores.

5. Product-level forecasting helps identify products with relatively higher expected demand.

6. The Random Forest model uses store, item, and time-based features to estimate future demand.

7. The predicted demand can support inventory planning by helping businesses prioritize products and stores according to expected demand.

8. The forecast results are exported into `foresight_inventory_analysis.csv`, which can be used for further analysis and Power BI dashboard development.
